STL → CAD Converter

Converts a triangle mesh (STL) into a watertight solid B-rep and exports it as STEP (+ BREP). Siemens NX imports the STEP and turns it into a native Parasolid body, so no Parasolid SDK is needed.

Methods

  • faceted (default) — every mesh triangle becomes a face. Always produces a closed solid with zero deviation from the mesh, but the STEP file is large (one face per triangle).
  • arcwise (experimental) — segments the mesh, fits real plane/cylinder/cone/sphere/torus surfaces plus B-spline patches, and rebuilds a compact B-rep (~1 face per feature). Currently may leave a few open edges — import the STEP as a sheet body and run one Sew/Heal in NX.

Parameters

Analytic tolmax distance (mm) for a patch to be accepted as an exact plane/cylinder/… — lower = stricter
Decimate totarget triangle count after cleanup. Big STLs must come down; ~20–40 k is a good range
Dihedralregion-growing angle limit (°). Lower splits at gentler edges (more, smaller patches)
Arc simplifyhow aggressively boundary curves are straightened (arcwise only)

Reading the result

By type — colours each face by the surface it was fitted to, so you can see at a glance what got recognised: plane, cylinder, cone, sphere, torus, B-spline / freeform. Turn on Wireframe with it to inspect patch boundaries.

Deviation — colours the input mesh by its distance to the reconstructed solid: blue = on the surface, green = half the tolerance, red = at or beyond the tolerance.

Limits

Very dense meshes (e.g. a fine grille with > ~90 k irreducible triangles) are refused — decimate them in your mesh tool first. Everything runs locally; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
